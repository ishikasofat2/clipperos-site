ClipperOS

ClipperOS is a private tool used by the Clutch Cuts channels (YouTube @clutchcuts37) to edit short highlight clips from streams that creators have licensed for clipping through official clipping campaigns, and to publish them to the channel owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts.

It is operated by an individual developer for their own channels only. It does not offer accounts to the public and has no other users.
