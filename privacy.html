Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

ClipperOS is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram/Facebook accounts. It has no other users.

What data we access

With the owner's permission, ClipperOS uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to, and read view counts of, the owner's own channel; and the Meta Graph API to publish reels and read view counts for the owner's own accounts.

How we use and store it

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private servers and are used only to upload the owner's videos and read their statistics. We do not collect data about other people, we do not sell or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

ClipperOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ClipperOS uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for the owner's own reporting. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored tokens are deleted on request or when access is revoked.
